## Photographs supplied by the business

The four photographs in the home page's "Craft, up close" band were
taken by the business and supplied for the site (28 Nov 2026). Each is
cropped to the goods alone, with shelf price strips, store signage and
other products left out. Each crop was then enhanced (29 Nov 2026) with
Seedream 5.0 Pro (rugs, vessels, tableware) or Arena's "Max" router (tray) on
arena.ai: upscaled, sharpened and cleaned of noise, with the prompt requiring
the same subject, composition, colours and motifs and nothing added. For the
tableware the model was also asked to remove three hanging price tags. Each
result was checked against its crop, then saved at up to 1600 px wide with a
half-size `-small` copy. The `-hd` file names are new so browsers holding the
earlier photos fetch these.
