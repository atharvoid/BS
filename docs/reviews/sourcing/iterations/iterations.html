01 / Textiles & fabrics

T1 · material selection

Fanned fabric swatches

A compact sourcing swatch set with visible weave and a small binding ring. More directly associated with fabric selection than a stack of folded lengths.

T2 · fabric drape

Draped sample rail

Three hanging lengths with a curved over-rail fold and gently uneven hems. The silhouette emphasises cloth and drape rather than a padded or stacked object.

02 / Agriculture & food

A1 · commodity sampling

Open commodity scoops

Low sampling vessels with handles and contents above their edges. A trade-focused alternative to the stepped display holder, without sacks or tall containers.

A2 · simple food ingredients

Low sampling bowls

A quieter, rounded cluster with no display plinth or handles. Easier visual grouping at small sizes, but less specifically connected to commodity trading than the scoops.
